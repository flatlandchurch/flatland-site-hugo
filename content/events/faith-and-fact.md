This six-week series explores how scripture and science, often seen as contradictory, both reveal God. Through God’s speech to Job in the Bible, we'll confront the tension between faith and intellect, examine God's work in the universe, and see how ancient scripture remains relevant today. Pursuing truth through revelation and research, we’ll ultimately discover that wonder, reason, and faith all point us to our Creator.
