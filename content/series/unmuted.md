What if your story could help someone else find Jesus? In Unmuted, we’ll follow six everyday people in Acts who helped advance the gospel. They didn’t have it all together, but they said yes to God. As we explore their stories, you’ll be challenged to step past fear and hesitation and begin living a faith that’s meant to be shared.
